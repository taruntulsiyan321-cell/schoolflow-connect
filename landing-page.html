Core Capabilities

Everything You Need to Run a School

Five role-based dashboards. One unified platform. Built for Indian curricula (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards) with bilingual support.

Academic Management

Timetables, curriculum mapping (RBSE/CBSE), lesson plans, homework, exams, and marks — all aligned to Indian board standards with Devanagari support.

AI Learning Analytics

Real-time dashboards for principals and teachers. Weak-area detection, concept mastery tracking, and predictive performance modeling per student.
